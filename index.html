01

Fire and Swirls

I made this fire by stacking layers: a black-to-white gradient, a Clouds effect for the flame texture, and then colors to turn it red and orange. I also tried different color settings to make blue and pink-green versions, and each one looks completely different.

Gradient · Clouds · Swirl
02

Fluffy Clouds

I built these clouds with soft white shapes on a gradient sky, then blurred them so they look fluffy. I made a blue daytime sky and a purple-pink sunset sky to see how the background color changes the mood.

Clouds Effect · Layers
03

Cool Explosion / Exploding Planet

I used a planet texture and added effects like Radial Blur to make it look like it is blasting apart, with light streaking out from the center. I made two color versions, a natural green and orange one and a bright orange and purple one.

Effects · Layers · Blend
04

Blend Modes

I changed the Blend Mode on my layers to see how they mix together, and the colors turned glitchy and rainbow. I used this lesson to try the examples in the documentation and then build my own version with a helmeted character and a big-eared creature behind it.

Blend Modes · Layer Properties
05

Poster

For my poster, I made a collage for Project Hail Mary using slanted strips of space and planet scenes, with the astronaut in the middle and the title at the bottom. It combines skills from earlier projects, like layers, cutting out a subject, and adding text.

Text · Layout · Effects
